## The problem (official)
combinatorprize.org: "Is the S combinator on its own computation universal?" — prove or disprove
Wolfram's conjecture (7 Dec 2020) that S alone (rule `S x y z -> x z (y z)`) is universal. $20,000.
Submission = publishable paper; "definite, precise results and arguments" required.

Wolfram's announcement (2021-06) adds: a direct input→output (normal form) representation cannot work;
universality must "piggy-back" on **non-halting** evolutions via an **encoder, detector, decoder** that are
themselves not universal (ideally provably halting in bounded time). Multiway: strong version =
emulate a nondeterministic TM's multiway system; weak version = some evaluation path performs any
deterministic computation. Non-universality routes: finitely many forms / modulations die out /
evolution "solvable" by a bounded computation. An "intermediate degree" (undecidable but not universal)
would also count as a resolution.

## Strategic picture
* Normal forms (Waldmann) and head normal forms (Padovani) are decidable ⇒ neither can be the halting
  signal of a universal encoding. Universality, if true, must live in **iterated** head normalization:
  the Böhm tree (strategy-independent, by confluence) or in non-conversion-invariant features of a
  fixed evaluation order.
* R6 shows Böhm trees of S-terms are not finite-state objects; each node is computed by a
  fast (conj. linear-time) head normalization, so all complexity sits in the *Böhm-tree dynamics*
  `t ↦ components of hnf(t)`. This is the most promising arena for a universality construction
  ("BT-spine computation": configuration term X_i → hnf `S X_{i+1} G_{i+1}`, garbage G emitted sideways,
  halting = spine becomes finite; leftmost spine ⇒ also realized by leftmost-outermost evaluation).
* Non-universality would require controlling BT dynamics — R6 makes the simplest route (regular BTs) false,
  but a "HORS-like" picture (all MSO properties of BTs decidable via finite S-models) remains open and is
  consistent with all data. Theorem E (recognizer counter-free; HN consequence conditional on Q10) and C4 (BT-finiteness
  counter-free, strongly supported) both favour H1: the finitary verdicts of S-dynamics cannot count, even
  though Böhm-tree *shapes* can (Theorem C). See NEXT_STEPS.md.
